What to Avoid (Absorption Rule):

Do not drink tea or coffee right after meals. The tannins bind with iron and block your body from absorbing it by up to 60%. Keep a 1-hour gap.

Blood Tests to Ask Your Doctor:
  • Complete Blood Count (CBC): Measures exact Hemoglobin (Hb) levels.
  • Serum Ferritin: Measures stored iron in your body.
  • Serum Vitamin B12: Confirms B12 levels if tingling/fatigue continues.
